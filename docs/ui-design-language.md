# KYS Chess UI Design Language

Apply when adding or changing chess screens, layouts, or interactions. Use preparation (整備) as the visual baseline: **warm, translucent, readable, with complete overviews.**

## Visuals

- Keep the **1280×720 logical canvas**. All game UI uses this fixed coordinate system, regardless of physical window or device size. The presentation layer scales the complete canvas and maps pointer coordinates; do not add alternate layouts, font sizes, column counts, or pagination based on viewport dimensions. ImGui tooling is exempt.
- Reuse panels, text, icons, and control states from [ChessUiStyle.h](../src/ChessUiStyle.h). Warm charcoal panels reveal the scene beneath. Current alpha values: panels 180/255, full-screen scrim 48/255; avoid arbitrary darkening per screen.
- Use ivory for body text, muted taupe for secondary text, gold for emphasis and primary actions, cyan for contextual information, and green/red for positive/negative states. Preserve semantic colors for piece tiers, stars, gold, experience, and difficulty.
- Default type sizes: page title 24, section title 22, body 18, metadata 16; dense tables may use 14. Simplify columns and wording before shrinking text.
- Preparation uses 26 px for its page title, 24 px section headings, 20 px controls and 19 px paginated descriptions within the existing panel geometry. Dense roster names remain 18 px (14 px on the compact bench), with a shaded name strip when bench names are visible. Preparation and synergy artwork is fitted to its visible alpha bounds at load time, preserving aspect ratio and existing control bounds.
- Default spacing: outer margin 16, section gap 12, padding 24; panel/button corner radii 6/4. Dense reports may reduce padding while retaining alignment and consistent line spacing.

## Information and Interaction

- Keep the main list, board, or comparison visible. Hover previews; clicking pins the selection. Prefer expanding details within the same right-hand inspector (檢視器).
- Complex content may open an existing full view, such as the synergy overview. Back must restore the originating screen and selection, including its page, category/search filter, and keyboard focus. Keep this position when rebuilding menus after actions or challenge battles; avoid duplicate subviews.
- Use readable, color-coded, single-screen tables for comparisons such as talents. Organize long guides and challenges with tab buttons and ample content space.
- Pair shared icons with short labels. Distinguish Back from Return to Start; show hover, selection, focus, and disabled states clearly.
- Preparation controls fit icons up to 36 px inside their existing bounds and use short labels such as 包、鑑、改、存、讀 and 禁. Synergy counts use up to 22 px, fitted within their chip. Desktop tooltips appear after 325 ms and equipment previews after 175 ms. On mobile, hold an icon for 350 ms to preview its tooltip; releasing after a hold or sliding off consumes the action. Short taps retain their existing actions.
- Preparation cards slide to their destination over 220 ms; keep existing display order and append arriving pieces to the end of the destination group. This display order does not change battlefield formation. The tutorial uses a cyan outline restricted to the highlighted panels, excluding the inspector from the deployment/bench step. Show the luck badge only for the 賭運 talent.
- Banning uses two actions: double-click/double-tap a character card in ban selection, or select a character and press 封禁. Submit immediately without another confirmation or success popup; update the banned card and remaining count in place. Keep banned cards in their original positions with the shared red outline and disable further bans, including the Gambler's opening selection and reward bans. Shop portrait double-click still purchases. Retain the separate confirmation when abandoning unused reward bans.
- Expose frequent actions directly, using the bottom action bar for discoverable save/load controls. Avoid nested “More → menu → menu” flows. List/map formation returns to the main pre-battle formation menu.
- In preparation, clicking a coin price buys immediately on desktop and mobile; purchases fill available deployment slots automatically. Portraits inspect on one click and buy or move on double click. The saved icon-only movement switch changes owned-piece movement to one click, with selected styling showing the active state. Preparation has no swap interaction: moving a piece into a full destination shows 出戰已滿 or 候補已滿 and leaves the roster unchanged. The player must free a destination slot before moving. The session enforces both deployment and bench capacities. The question-mark icon opens a guided overlay on the actual preparation UI, highlighting real prices, roster controls and actions. Guided actions use the current game and gold; explain this in the purchase step. Keep the instructional popup clear of the highlighted controls, allow only the current step's actions, and offer next/previous/close without requiring a purchase.
- Show static content and a meaningful loading notice first; block dependent actions until ready. Do not assume background threads are available.
- Use 24 px cyan waiting text, a readable current stage, and stage progress for pre-battle loading. Keep the proceed action visibly unavailable until loading completes.
- The saved “戰前點擊任意處繼續” setting defaults off. When enabled, a fresh mouse click or touch after the ready frame continues from anywhere in the pre-battle overview, replacing pointer inspection. Show the active behavior and explain how to restore inspection; preserve the formation step when enabled. Reuse the shared pointer activation sequence and loading input reset so held touches cannot advance.
- Action sections over scenery use one shallow translucent panel behind the whole group: hints, navigation and confirmation actions. This includes selection footers, tab rows, formation headers, and pre-/post-battle footers. Reuse an existing enclosing panel where present; do not add backgrounds or change colors on individual buttons.
- Reserve space for action bars in the content layout. Keep a 12 px gap from adjacent panels, center controls vertically, and leave padding between their focus outlines and the bar edges.
- Write in-game copy in Traditional Chinese and describe actual chess rules. Remove unused fields, empty-effect explanations, and redundant labels. Group related stats and keep values close to their names.

## Battle Overviews

- **In battle:** hover previews and clicking pins a unit; clicking empty battlefield space or pressing Cancel dismisses it. Do not show a lock label or close button. Keep identity, equipment, skills, HP/MP bars, cooldown, shield, live attributes, and damage dealt/taken in a compact fixed bottom panel, grouped into identity/equipment, resources, and combat stats. Use slim, flat green HP and blue MP bars with labels and values above the fills; keep cooldown/shield as a quiet metadata row. Align attribute and damage labels/values on a shared grid, separated from resources and identity by the shared subtle divider. Buffs/debuffs occupy a separate lower-left panel that grows upward; prioritize timed effects, then consumable shields/charges, then ongoing modifiers. Mark each card 限時 / 可消耗 / 常駐 using runtime lifetime semantics, preserving buff/debuff colors. Combine source and duration in compact metadata rows; paginate complete effect cards when the full height is exhausted. Both panels capture input so gestures do not move the battlefield behind them. Battle controls use the generated icons in `chess-preparation.zip`.
- **Pre-battle:** preserve both complete rosters with names, stars, HP, attack, defense, speed (輕功), equipment, and synergy thresholds. Skill mechanics and rules can live in the inspector.
- **Post-battle:** make a shareable report with up to 13 units per side on one screen, one row per unit, including defeated units and clones. Retain damage dealt, DPS, damage taken, kills, damage canceled (抵消), remaining HP, equipment names, and the two main skills with their damage.
- Stack teams vertically with shared column boundaries and right-aligned numbers. Place skill damage beside its name. Keep equipment names alongside icons.
- Each team's right-hand summary shows its talent, around eight inner skills (內功), and synergies (羈絆) **that reach the first threshold**. Normally show icon, name, and threshold; dense sets may use multiple columns of icons and thresholds.
- Offline battles show both players' talents. Omit effect tutorials, duration in seconds, and screenshot-saving controls from the report. Open logs explicitly and return to the same report; clicking the report body must not dismiss it.
